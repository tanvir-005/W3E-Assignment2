EAGLE CREEK COURSE FEATURES

Practice Facilities

  • Driving range with target greens
  • Putting green
  • Short game area with sand bunkers
  • Professional lessons available

Amenities and Services

Caddies and Golf Cart Rentals
  • Availability: Golf carts are equipped with GPS and available for rental. Caddie services are available upon request.
  • Costs: Cart rentals: $25; Caddie fees: $40/hour.
On-Course Food and Beverage
  • Multiple snack bars and a halfway house are conveniently located. Beverage carts circulate regularly.

When is the Best Time of Year to Play at Eagle Creek Golf Club?

Fall and Spring are the best seasons to play at Eagle Creek Golf Club. During these months, the weather is mild with minimal rain, providing perfect golfing conditions. Winter is also a great option for those seeking year-round play in Florida's warmer climate.
